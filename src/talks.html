Students learned SMILES/SELFIES, RDKit parsing, physicochemical properties (logP, TPSA, MW), and 2D visualisation — building SMILES → molecule → property-table pipelines.

Class 2

Fingerprints, Similarity & Intro GNNs

Cheminformatics · Institute of Science Tokyo (internal)

Bioinformatics · University of Tsukuba (external)

End-to-end workflows: sanitisation, descriptor engineering, QSAR-style supervised learning, scaffold-split evaluation; Jupyter notebooks for drug discovery and environmental toxicology.
