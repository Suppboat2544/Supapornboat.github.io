Workshops & Teaching

Class 1

Molecular Representations & RDKit Foundations

Cheminformatics · Institute of Science Tokyo (internal)

SMILES/SELFIES, RDKit parsing, physicochemical properties (logP, TPSA, MW), and 2D visualisation — SMILES → molecule → property-table pipelines.

Class 2

Fingerprints, Similarity & Intro GNNs

Cheminformatics · Institute of Science Tokyo (internal)

Morgan/ECFP fingerprints, Tanimoto search, PCA/UMAP chemical space, and introductory graph neural networks for molecular property prediction.

Class 3

Graph Theory Foundations for Satellite Datasets

JAXA · Graph Theory for Satellite & Stellar Data

Nodes, edges, paths, connectivity; casting Earth-observation and satellite metadata as spatial grids, orbit–ground links, and sensor co-occurrence networks.

Class 4

Graph Algorithms on Satellite Networks

JAXA · Graph Theory for Satellite & Stellar Data

Shortest paths, centrality, community detection, graph Laplacians; NetworkX analysis of constellation and spatial-neighbourhood graphs.

Class 5

Stellar Project — Graph Methods for Stellar Data

JAXA · Graph Theory for Satellite & Stellar Data

Similarity graphs for star catalogues, stellar-population clustering, community visualisation; mini Stellar Project deliverable.

Class 6

Marine Metagenomics & Ocean Sequence Pipelines

Marine Bioinformatics · JAMSTEC

Ocean-derived FASTA (eDNA/metagenomes), QC, k-mer features, and microbial diversity across depth and geographic zones.

Class 7

Protein Language Models for Marine Enzymes

Marine Bioinformatics · JAMSTEC

ESM-2 embedding extraction, batch inference, and evolutionary/structural signal for ocean enzyme discovery.

Class 8

Marine Enzyme Family Discovery & Environmental Clustering

Marine Bioinformatics · JAMSTEC

UMAP by ocean context, hierarchical DBSCAN/HDBSCAN, Silhouette/EC consistency, and ecological interpretation.

Class 9

Applied Cheminformatics for Molecular Discovery

Invited Lecturer · University of Liverpool (external)

Sanitisation, descriptor engineering, QSAR-style supervised learning, scaffold-split evaluation; Jupyter notebooks for drug discovery and environmental toxicology.

Class 10

Bioinformatics Foundations for Sequence Data

Invited Lecturer · University of Liverpool (external)

FASTA/preprocessing, k-mers, alignment intuition, and embedding-based representations for biological sequences.

Class 11

Protein Language Models & Biological Clustering

Invited Lecturer · University of Liverpool (external)

ESM-2-style embeddings, UMAP, and clustering workflows for biological discovery; interpreting embedding spaces and cluster quality.

Class 12

Integrating Cheminformatics & Bioinformatics for Molecular Insight

Invited Lecturer · University of Liverpool (external)

Capstone: ligand–target mapping, structure–sequence relationships, and joint RDKit + sequence-embedding discovery case studies.
