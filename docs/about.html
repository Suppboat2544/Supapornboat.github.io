Hi, I'm Boat

I am a Ph.D. student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the Institute of Science Tokyo, supervised by Prof. Manabu Fujii (Fujii Lab). My doctoral research focuses on AI-Driven Generation of Carboxyl-Rich Aliphatic/Alicyclic Molecules (CRAM) — a property-conditioned deep learning approach for understanding refractory dissolved organic matter — alongside developing the CRAM-GTransformer, a bidirectional graph transformer for site-specific and pathway-level CRAM biotransformation prediction.

With extensive experience in data analysis using Python, R, and Julia, I am passionate about leveraging AI to solve complex environmental challenges — from PFAS contamination and marine carbon cycling to biotransformation pathway prediction and kinetic modeling.

Current Research

Open to new collaborations — click any topic to learn more. Research interests also span other pages of this site.

01
CRAM Generation & DOM Understanding ＋

AI-Driven Generation of Carboxyl-Rich Aliphatic/Alicyclic Molecules (CRAM): A Property-Conditioned Deep Learning Approach for Understanding Refractory Dissolved Organic Matter.

Constructing a chemical space refinement framework to decode the structural complexity of marine DOM. Applying graph-based filtering, network analysis, and HR-MS/MS spectral matching. Building a property-conditioned generative model (CRAM) with VAE + GAT encoder and GRU-SELFIES decoder. Enhanced median candidate isomers per deep-ocean molecular formula from 1.4 (PubChem-only) to 9.3.

View CRAM project → Contact for collaboration →
02
CRAM-GTransformer: Biotransformation Prediction ＋

Bidirectional Graph Transformer for Site-Specific and Pathway-Level CRAM Biotransformation — predicting multigenerational transformation of refractory DOM.

Published GSAT model achieving R² = 0.907, 98.9% faster than traditional methods. Analyzed 4.7 million reaction steps across 12 priority pollutants — 68.2% of metabolic transformations increase toxicity by an average 15.4-fold. Currently building CRAM-GTransformer with 210K+ reaction pairs, 87% Top-3 accuracy, trained across 99 biotransformation classes.

View GSAT model → Contact for collaboration →
03
Identification of MS1-Formula Assignment ＋

Building computational pipelines for molecular formula assignment from MS1 mass spectrometry data.

Developing pipelines for accurate identification of unknown compounds in complex environmental samples. Applying mass difference network algorithms for automated PFAS transformation product identification and high-throughput profiling. Integrating HR-MS/MS spectral matching with chemical space refinement for unknown compound discovery in marine and freshwater DOM.

All research topics → Contact for collaboration →
04
Kinetic Reaction Deep Learning Model ＋

Applying deep learning to predict kinetic rate constants and reaction dynamics for environmental chemical transformations.

Developing a hybrid GAT + MPNN architecture for predicting environmental persistence of organic compounds. Integrated 200+ molecular descriptors including 3D conformational features (ETKDG), achieving R²=0.847. Built Physics-Informed Neural Networks (R²=0.93) for predicting chemical degradation across 0–6000m ocean depth; discovered 17,500× half-life variation from surface to abyssal waters. Validated against ARGO/GLODAP oceanographic databases (300K+ measurements).

All research topics → Contact for collaboration →
05
Clustering Techniques in High-Dimensional Datasets ＋

Developing and applying advanced clustering algorithms to extract structure from high-dimensional chemical and biological data.

Applying hierarchical DBSCAN, UMAP-assisted dimensionality reduction, and graph-partitioning methods to cluster large-scale molecular and sequence datasets — including 1.29M marine enzyme sequences (ESM-2 embeddings + k-mer features) and chemical space networks for DOM. Investigating optimal distance metrics and density-based approaches for sparse, heterogeneous molecular feature spaces where traditional Euclidean distances break down. Interested in collaborations involving large omics datasets, metabolomics, or chemical library clustering.
