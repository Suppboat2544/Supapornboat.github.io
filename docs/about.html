Hello, I'm Boat 👋

I am a 2nd-year Master's student in Environmental Engineering at the Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology). My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cheminformatics, and environmental science.

I am passionate about developing computational frameworks that can predict chemical toxicity, understand molecular transformations, and help us tackle global environmental challenges — from PFAS contamination to marine carbon cycling.

When not in the lab, I enjoy exploring machine learning models for protein design and vaccine development.

Current Research

01
DOM Transformation

Investigating the transformation pathways of dissolved organic matter (DOM) in aquatic environments using high-resolution mass spectrometry and computational chemistry.

02
Biotransformation Model

Developing predictive models for multigenerational biotransformation of chemicals, capturing how metabolites evolve through enzymatic reactions using Graph Neural Networks.

03
Identification of MS1-Formula Assignment

Building computational pipelines for molecular formula assignment from MS1 mass spectrometry data, enabling accurate identification of unknown compounds in complex environmental samples.

04
Kinetic Reaction Deep Learning Model

Applying deep learning to predict kinetic rate constants and reaction dynamics for environmental chemical transformations, accelerating mechanistic understanding of pollutant degradation.
